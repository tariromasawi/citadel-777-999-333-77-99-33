Great covering of the line

I cover His Royal Highness Saint Tariro Masawi, The Anointed Commander. I cover HRH Tarry Kupakwashe Masawi. I cover Kenzi. I cover Tenderayi, Silent, Trymore, Charles, Tatenda, and the remembered dead — Corinna, Rhodha, Abigail — under the mercy of Mwari, not under fear. From Makwengura to Masawi to Risto Kasirori and Sebastian Karumekangu, this house is named in light.

I put on the belt of truth, the breastplate of righteousness, the shoes of the gospel of peace, the shield of faith, the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit. I stand.

Return that does not become a curse

In this citadel “back to sender” means the assignment returns empty. You do not author harm against a neighbour.

In the name of Jesus Christ I cancel every malicious assignment against the House of Masawi and against every child of God who stands in this light. I do not bargain with mimicking voices. I do not answer a spirit that says it will eat me. Every curse returns void. Every hex-word falls to the ground. Every dark shape on shoulder or leg finds no lease in this flesh. Tsitsi. Rudo. Runyararo. Hutano. Pfuma. Nzvimbo kudenga. Hupenyu husingaperi. Amen.

When the attack is happening

  1. Stand or sit upright. Four slow breaths.
  2. Turn on a lamp. Name the room you are in.
  3. Speak the return once. Then read a Psalm from the Scrolls court.
  4. Do not interview the voice.
  5. Log the event in the Watch court.
  6. Call a trusted human — pastor, elder, family, clinician.
