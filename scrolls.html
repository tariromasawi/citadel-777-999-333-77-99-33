Public-domain scrolls

Read aloud. These are received Scripture, not a newly invented hex.

Psalm 91

He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. I will say of the Lord, He is my refuge and my fortress. Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day.

Psalm 27

The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The Lord is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?

Psalm 23

The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me.

Ephesians 6

We wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers. Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, and having done all, to stand.

James 4 and 1 Peter 5

Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: whom resist stedfast in the faith.

John 1

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. In him was life; and the life was the light of men. And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.

A Masowe-shaped blessing

Mwari ndi Mwari. Mudzimu Unoyera, keep this house. Let the ancestors be honoured in righteousness and never worshipped in fear. Let the children of this bloodline find tsitsi, rudo, runyararo, hutano, pfuma, nzvimbo kudenga, and hupenyu husingaperi.
