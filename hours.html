Eight watches

The day is divided so panic does not become the only liturgy. If the night is heavy, use Compline and then sleep as an act of trust.

Lauds · dawn

This is the day which the Lord hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it. — Psalm 118

I open the house to light. Tsitsi at the first hour.

Terce · mid-morning

Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path. — Psalm 119

Work under covering. Rudo in speech and in the hands.

Sext · noon

The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. — Psalm 23

Hutano at the height of the day. Eat. Drink water.

None · afternoon

My help cometh from the Lord, which made heaven and earth. — Psalm 121

Pfuma without anxiety. The devourer is refused.

Vespers · evening

Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense. — Psalm 141

Runyararo as the sun falls. Name the living heirs in blessing.

Compline · night

I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety. — Psalm 4

I close the doors. I do not interview the dark.

Vigil · if you wake

He that keepeth thee will not slumber. — Psalm 121

Rise, light the room, speak the return once, then return to Compline.

Lord’s Day · assembly

I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Lord. — Psalm 122

Do not fight this battle only alone.
